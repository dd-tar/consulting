How I work

Small first. Bigger only if it earns it.

Step one

We talk

30 minutes, free. You describe the task in your own words; I ask the annoying-but-necessary questions.

Step two

Diagnostic sprint

1–2 weeks, fixed price, small scope, real deliverable. You see how I think before committing to anything big.

Step three

Project or advisory

Ongoing work, a defined project, or a few hours a month of a translator on call — whatever the task actually needs.

Every engagement ends in a document you own — not a memory of a nice call.

No jargon in deliverables. If your grandmother can't follow the summary, I rewrite it.

If you don't need me, I'll say so on the first call. It's cheaper for both of us.
