The symptoms

Sound familiar?

  • You're about to spend real money on a product, system, or vendor — and can't yet say precisely what you need.
  • Every stakeholder describes the problem differently. The meetings multiply; the decision doesn't.
  • Build it, buy it, fix the process instead — or do nothing? Someone has to actually find out.
  • The vendor's deck says "proprietary technology". Nobody in the room can check.

Then you don't need more opinions. You need someone to go find out — and come back with a decision you can defend.

Fit

Let's save each other a call

Work with me if

  • You're about to commit budget to something that isn't fully defined yet.
  • Your stakeholders don't agree on what the problem is — and you need them to.
  • A vendor, technology, or market needs an independent read before you sign.
  • You're an agency that wants discovery done with your client before the quote.

Don't, if

  • You've already decided and want research that agrees.
  • You need confidence delivered regardless of what the data says.
  • You need a full-time employee, not a scoped engagement.
  • The answer has to arrive before the research does.

Questions

Asked before you ask

What does a first engagement look like?

A free 30-minute call, then a fixed-price Decision & Discovery Sprint of one to two weeks: small scope, real deliverable. Bigger work only after that, and only if the sprint earned it.

We already have a dev team / an agency. Where do you fit?

Before them, mostly. I define what to build and why; they build it. Agencies also bring me in directly to run discovery with their clients before quoting a project — white-label is fine.

Can you help with AI adoption?

Yes — an AI decision is still a decision. I build with LLMs daily, so I can tell you where AI is the right answer and where a database and two good queries would do. It shows up inside sprints and assessments, not as a separate "AI strategy" package.

What if the research contradicts what we hoped for?

You'll hear it, with the evidence. Every claim in my deliverables is labeled: known, assumed, or unknown — and what experiment would settle it. I don't sell conviction.

Do you write code?

I can read it, test technical claims hands-on, and I ship my own products — that's what makes the assessment work credible. I don't take contract engineering roles.

What languages and time zones?

English and Russian, both at working depth, deliverables included. Remote, worldwide, anchored in Central European Time.
